We build AI that makes infrastructure safer, smarter, and more resilient.

PIVAI develops AI-driven inspection technologies that redefine how infrastructure is monitored and maintained. We design complete systems - from sensing hardware to AI models and data pipelines - that turn field data into reliable engineering insights.

Our Mission

To enable safer, smarter, and more resilient infrastructure through real-time intelligence. We deliver actionable insights directly from the field, reducing reliance on manual inspections and accelerating maintenance decisions.

Proprietary Technologies

PIVAI’s platform integrates sensor hardware, edge AI, and scalable data infrastructure into a unified system. From acquisition to inference to reporting, every component is engineered to convert raw data into actionable intelligence, in real time.
