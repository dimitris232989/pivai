SignMap

SignMap leverages computer vision, multi-sensor data, and edge AI to detect, classify, and evaluate infrastructure assets in real time. The system identifies traffic signs, assesses visibility and condition, and detects pavement defects such as cracks and potholes — all while operating on moving vehicles.

  • Real-time traffic sign detection, classification, and condition assessment
  • Automated crack and pothole detection with geospatial tagging
  • Database validation and asset inventory synchronization
  • Heatmaps for roadway condition and maintenance prioritization
  • Seamless integration with GIS and asset management systems
