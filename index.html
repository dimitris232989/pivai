About Pivai

PIVAI builds intelligent inspection systems that combine AI, advanced sensors, and edge computing to solve real-world engineering challenges. Our technology transforms how infrastructure is monitored, converting raw visual data into structured, actionable intelligence in real time. By integrating hardware, software, and machine learning into a unified platform, we enable faster decisions, reduced operational costs, and scalable deployment across transportation and environmental systems.
